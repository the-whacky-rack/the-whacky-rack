About Us

We started as a group chat. We became a cult. We're now a website. Nobody is more surprised about this than us.

The short version

The Whacky Rack is run by four co-owners. Three of them call themselves The Three Musketeers and have been best friends since 2018, despite never having met in person. The fourth, D'Artagnan, is a sleeping partner whose actual role remains a mystery even to us.

1How it started

We started as a cult. Not the kind with robes and a compound — though we're open to it if the Wi-Fi's good — but the kind that gathers at 2 AM to send each other links to inflatable dinosaur costumes and say "look at this" with no further context.

The idea was simple: collect the weirdest things the internet has to offer, put them in one place, and let people scroll through them the way our ancestors scrolled through cave walls looking for meaning. Same energy. Better lighting.

We had no business plan. We had no budget. We had one group chat, three people who'd never met in person, and an unreasonable number of opinions about whether a hot dog toaster was "peak innovation" or "a war crime."

Reader, we built the site.

2The cult

We are, technically, a cult. Not a scary one. A cult the way a book club is a cult, if the book club spent its evenings ranking novelty socks and arguing about whether a raccoon-shaped nightlight counts as "essential home décor."

Anyone can join. Anyone can browse. Anyone can forward a link to a friend with the caption "you need this" and then pretend it wasn't them.

But if you're going to call yourself a member — a real member — we have three rules:

  1. You must have, at some point, laughed alone at a product description.
  2. You must have sent someone a link to a screaming goat at an inappropriate hour.
  3. You must accept, deep in your bones, that you do not need any of this. You will want all of it.

If that's you, welcome home. If that's not you yet, give it a week.

3The Three Musketeers (and one mystery)

The Whacky Rack is run by four co-owners. Three of them call themselves The Three Musketeers, because none of them thought of a better name in time and now it's load-bearing.

They are Aramis, Athos, and Porthos — friends since 2018, inseparable ever since, and yet none of them have ever met in person. Not once. Not even for a coffee. It turns out you don't need to be in the same country to build a deep, unshakeable friendship based entirely on sending each other increasingly weird Amazon listings.

Then there's D'Artagnan.

We don't know much about D'Artagnan. We know he exists. We know he's a sleeping partner. We know that if you ask three people at The Whacky Rack what D'Artagnan does, you get three different answers, none of which are correct.

That's the whole bio. We're as confused as you are.

5What we actually do

Three things, loosely:

  • Find weird products — the kind you don't need but immediately want, from inflatable hot dog costumes to novelty candles.
  • Collect world oddities — real festivals, strange laws, and traditions from every corner of the globe that nobody can quite explain.
  • Dig up weird history — true stories from the past that sound completely invented but aren't.

We're not a shop. We're not a media company. We're three friends and one mystery partner who decided to build something for people who also think "who even thought of this?" is the highest form of compliment.

Come in. Sit down. Scroll.

You don't need any of this. You never did. But here you are, and we're glad. If you've found something truly weird and want to share it, we'd love to see it.
