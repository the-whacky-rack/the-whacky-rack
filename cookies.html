Cookie Policy

We keep cookies to an absolute minimum. There are no advertising trackers, no analytics scripts, and no profiling. We set no first-party cookies. The only cookies on this site come from Amazon when you click an affiliate link, and from a social platform if you use the share button.

Last updated: 2026

Plain-English summary

The Whacky Rack itself sets no cookies. We do use your browser's localStorage to remember which products you've liked — this is not a cookie and is never sent to our servers automatically. When you click an Amazon affiliate link, Amazon sets its own tracking cookie so it can credit a purchase to us. If you use a share button, the platform you choose may set its own cookies. You can clear cookies and localStorage at any time through your browser settings — the site will still work fine.

1What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, and track how visitors use a site.

Related technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels (also called web beacons). This policy covers all of them collectively as "cookies" for simplicity, though they are technically distinct and are treated separately below where it matters.

2Cookies we set ourselves

No cookies. The Whacky Rack does not set any first-party cookies, does not install analytics or advertising scripts, and does not run any form of cross-site tracking.

We do use your browser's localStorage for one user-facing purpose: remembering which products you've liked, and assigning your browser an anonymous identifier so that like counts can be tallied. Unlike cookies, localStorage data is never sent to any server automatically, and it is not used for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing for us to ask consent for on our own behalf. If we ever add analytics or a newsletter, we will update this page and add a consent mechanism where required.

4Services that do not set cookies

For clarity, the following services and technologies are used by the site but do not place cookies on your device:

  • Supabase — used to fetch product and story data, and to store like counts. Since we do not use Supabase authentication, no session cookies are created.
  • localStorage (our like feature) — we use your browser's localStorage to remember which products you've liked and to assign you an anonymous identifier. This is not a cookie, is not sent to any server automatically, and can be cleared at any time through your browser's site data settings.
  • ImgBB and other image hosts — serve product images. No cookies are set for anonymous image requests.
  • Our fonts and stylesheets — served from the same domain as the site.

5How to control cookies and localStorage

You have full control over cookies and localStorage through your browser settings. Common options include:

  • Blocking all third-party cookies.
  • Blocking cookies from specific sites.
  • Deleting existing cookies and clearing localStorage.
  • Browsing in private or incognito mode, which discards both when you close the window.

Here's how to manage cookies in the most common browsers:

Clearing your browser's site data or localStorage will remove the anonymous identifier we use and the record of products you've liked on this device. Your likes will reset to zero the next time you visit.

Blocking cookies will not break this site. It may, however, prevent Amazon from attributing a purchase to us if you later buy something — which simply means we don't earn a commission. It does not affect the price you pay.

6Do Not Track signals

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Because we do not track users ourselves and do not run analytics, there is nothing for us to disable in response. Amazon's and the social platforms' handling of DNT signals is governed by their own policies.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we add new features or services that use cookies. When that happens, we will update the "Last updated" date and — where required by law — ask for your consent before setting any new non-essential cookies.
