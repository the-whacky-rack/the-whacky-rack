Affiliate Disclosure

The Whacky Rack participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and other affiliate programs. This page explains, in plain English, exactly how our links work, how we earn money, and what it means for you as a reader.

Last updated: 2026

The short version

When you click certain product links on The Whacky Rack and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission. You pay the same price you would pay buying directly from the retailer. Commissions never influence which products we feature, and we never accept payment to write positive reviews. If you'd like to support the site, shopping through our links is a completely free way to do it.

1Who we are

The Whacky Rack is an independently operated editorial website that curates and writes about bizarre, funny, and wonderfully unnecessary products found on the internet. We are not a retailer, manufacturer, or marketplace. We do not sell any products directly.

To keep the site free to read and free of intrusive pop-up advertising, we participate in affiliate programs. These programs pay us a small commission when a reader clicks one of our links and completes a qualifying purchase.

2Amazon Associates Program

The Whacky Rack is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

This means that when you click a product link on The Whacky Rack that goes to Amazon.com and you purchase any qualifying item — not necessarily the item you clicked — within the 24-hour affiliate cookie window, we may receive a small commission from Amazon.

What qualifies as a purchase

  • Any item purchased on Amazon within the affiliate cookie window (typically 24 hours from your click).
  • Items added to your cart within the window, even if purchased later (up to 90 days, subject to Amazon's program terms).
  • Qualifying purchases must be completed through the Amazon.com US site to generate a commission.

What does not qualify

  • Gift cards and certain other excluded product categories.
  • Purchases made on international Amazon sites (Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.ca, etc.), unless we are separately enrolled in that region's program.
  • Returns, cancelled orders, or purchases made after the cookie window expires.

3What this costs you

Nothing. The commission we earn is paid by Amazon out of its own margin — it is not added to your price. You pay exactly the same amount you would pay if you navigated to Amazon.com directly and searched for the product yourself.

Shopping through our affiliate links is simply a way of saying "thanks" that costs you nothing extra. It helps us cover hosting, image hosting, and the time it takes to research and write about the products we feature.

4Editorial independence

Our editorial decisions are made independently of any affiliate relationship. Specifically:

  • We do not accept payment from Amazon or any brand in exchange for featuring a product.
  • We do not accept payment in exchange for a positive review, rating, or ranking.
  • Products are selected based on whether they are genuinely weird, funny, or interesting — not based on commission rates.
  • We may feature products that are not part of any affiliate program at all, simply because they belong on the site.
  • If a product we recommend turns out to be poor quality or discontinued, we remove it.

Our goal is to be a trusted source of genuinely entertaining product curation. Eroding that trust for a handful of commissions would defeat the entire purpose of the site.

5Product information & accuracy

Product prices, availability, images, descriptions, and reviews are provided by Amazon and are subject to change at any time without notice. While we aim to keep our listings accurate, the information shown on Amazon.com at the time of your purchase is the authoritative version — not the information shown on The Whacky Rack.

This means:

  • Prices may have changed since we last updated a listing.
  • Stock may have sold out or been discontinued.
  • Product specifications, packaging, or formulas may have been updated by the manufacturer.
  • Customer reviews and ratings on Amazon reflect the current state of that product, not necessarily the state at the time we wrote about it.

We are not responsible for any discrepancies between the information on this site and the information on Amazon.com. Always confirm details on Amazon before completing a purchase.

6FTC compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose the following:

  • The Whacky Rack has a material connection to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates program.
  • We receive financial compensation when readers click our Amazon links and make qualifying purchases.
  • This disclosure is provided clearly and conspicuously, near the top of every page that contains affiliate links, and in full on this page.
  • We do not make any claim, endorsement, or testimonial about a product unless we genuinely believe it to be true based on our own research.

If you have questions about FTC compliance as it relates to this site, you can read the FTC's full guidance at ftc.gov.

7Other affiliate programs

In addition to Amazon Associates, The Whacky Rack may participate in other affiliate networks and programs from time to time. The same principles apply across all of them:

  • We clearly mark affiliate links with the rel="nofollow sponsored" attribute.
  • All outbound commercial links open in a new tab.
  • Commissions never influence editorial coverage.
  • You pay the same price regardless of whether you use our link.

9Cookies & tracking

When you click an Amazon affiliate link on The Whacky Rack, Amazon may place a cookie on your device to track the referral. This is what allows Amazon to attribute a qualifying purchase back to our account and pay us the associated commission.

The Whacky Rack itself does not place tracking cookies, does not sell your personal data, and does not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us.

For details on Amazon's own data practices, please refer to the Amazon Privacy Notice.
