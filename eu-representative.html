Under GDPR Article 27

EU Representative

This page explains The Whacky Rack's position under Article 27 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which governs when a business based outside the EU must appoint a representative within the EU for data protection matters.

Last updated: 2026

1What Article 27 requires

GDPR Article 27 requires a data controller that is not established in the European Union but processes personal data of individuals in the EU to appoint a representative in a Member State where those individuals are located. That representative acts as a point of contact for data subjects and supervisory authorities for matters related to GDPR compliance.

Article 27(2) sets out exceptions. The obligation does not apply where the processing is occasional, does not include large-scale processing of special categories of personal data or criminal-offence data, and is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, taking into account the nature, context, scope, and purposes of the processing.

2Our position

The Whacky Rack is operated by a sole individual based in India. We process personal data of EU residents in the following limited way:

  • Account data for EU users who voluntarily create an account on the site — email address, optional profile details, and the content they post.
  • Anonymous analytics — page views and outbound link clicks recorded in our own database, only after the visitor has consented through our cookie banner.
  • Transactional emails — password reset links delivered by our email provider, sent only when a user requests them.

The processing is occasional (triggered by individual user actions, not systematic monitoring), is not large-scale, does not involve any special categories of personal data (such as health, ethnicity, political opinions, or biometric data), and does not involve criminal-offence data. We do not engage in behaviour tracking, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

We rely on the Article 27(2)(a) exemption. Given the occasional, non-large-scale, low-risk nature of our processing, we do not currently appoint an EU representative. We monitor this position as the site grows and will appoint a representative if our processing changes in a way that brings us within the mandatory scope.

3How to contact us

Even without a formal EU representative, we respond to GDPR requests from EU residents in full. If you wish to exercise any right under GDPR — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent — contact us directly:

  • Email: the.whacky.rack@limbomail.com
  • Response time: Within 30 days, as required by GDPR Article 12(3).
  • Language: English.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your EU Member State of residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.
