Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information The Whacky Rack collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect the minimum required to run accounts, community features, and moderation, we don't sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

Last updated: 2026

Plain-English summary

The Whacky Rack has user accounts and community features. To run them we collect your email address, a hashed password (managed by Supabase Auth), and whatever you choose to add to your profile and post. We do not collect real names, phone numbers, or payment details. We do not sell your data. The third parties that may set cookies or trackers on your device are Cloudflare (bot protection on signup, login, and password reset), Amazon (affiliate links), and social platforms (when you use the share button). You can delete your account at any time from your settings page.

1Who we are

The Whacky Rack ("we", "us", "our") is an independently operated editorial website that publishes curated product recommendations and factual stories from around the world, and hosts community features where users can post, comment, vote, and follow each other. We are the data controller for any personal information collected through this site.

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the.whacky.rack@limbomail.com.

2Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. The password is hashed and stored by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We never see your plaintext password. We also store the date you joined, a sequential rank number, and a randomly generated username (formats like Zany_Durian_45). Usernames cannot be changed after signup.

Profile information (optional)

You may optionally add a bio, country, gender, age, and an emoji avatar to your profile. You also declare that you are 18 or older at signup. We store your browser's IANA timezone so that timestamps display correctly for you.

User-generated content

Everything you post is stored on our servers and is publicly visible unless noted. This includes:

  • Yaps — posts you create about products, and any comments and replies underneath them (nested up to 6 levels deep).
  • Tapes — products you post to your personal timeline, with optional captions, plus any comments underneath them.
  • Votes and reactions — upvotes and downvotes on Yaps and comments, and your choice of reaction emoji (👍👎🤣🤬🎉💝) on Tapes. These are tied to your account so we can prevent double-voting.
  • Bookmarks — Yaps you save for later. Visible only to you.
  • Follows — accounts you follow and accounts that follow you. Visible to other logged-in users on followers pages.
  • Reports — reports you file against other users' content. Visible only to you and to moderators.
  • Notifications — a log of actions on your content (someone commented, reacted, followed, upvoted). Visible only to you.

Anonymous likes

On product cards, the "like" button works without an account. We store a randomly generated identifier (a UUID) in your browser's localStorage and send it, together with the product ID, to our database so the like can be counted. This identifier has no connection to your account, your name, or your email. If you later create an account, these anonymous likes remain separate from it.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting and backend providers record basic technical information when you visit — IP address, browser type, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. This is standard server-log data used for security, debugging, and understanding aggregate traffic patterns. It is not linked to your account by us.

Session data

After you sign in, Supabase Auth stores a session token in your browser's localStorage. This token authenticates your requests to the backend. It is not a cookie and is not sent automatically to third-party websites. Signing out clears it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect real names, phone numbers, or postal addresses.
  • We do not collect payment information — we don't sell anything directly.
  • We do not operate a marketing newsletter or mailing list. We only send transactional emails such as password reset links, and only when you request them.
  • We do not use session recording, cross-site tracking scripts, or browser fingerprinting.
  • We do not collect data from children under 13. The site is intended for adults 18+.

3How we use information

The information we collect is used only to:

  • Create and authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
  • Display your profile, Yaps, Tapes, comments, votes, and reactions to other users as intended by the community features.
  • Show you notifications when others interact with your content.
  • Enforce rate limits and prevent spam, bot signups, and abuse (including via Cloudflare Turnstile).
  • Review reports filed by users and take moderation action.
  • Send transactional emails you request (e.g. password reset links, delivered via Resend).
  • Understand aggregate, anonymised traffic patterns.
  • Respond to messages you send us.
  • Comply with legal obligations if required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

4Third parties who may receive data

Supabase (backend and authentication)

Supabase hosts our database and provides our authentication service. Your account credentials, profile data, and all user-generated content are stored on Supabase infrastructure. When you sign in, Supabase issues and validates session tokens. Supabase may log requests as part of normal infrastructure operation.

Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection)

Cloudflare Turnstile is used on the signup, login, and password reset forms to prevent automated account creation and credential-stuffing attacks. When you submit one of those forms, Cloudflare processes a challenge on your device. Cloudflare may set a short-lived challenge cookie and may log your IP address and user agent as part of the challenge. Turnstile is designed to be privacy-preserving and does not track you across sites.

Resend (transactional email)

We use Resend to deliver password reset emails. When you request a reset link, Resend receives your email address and the content of the message. We do not use Resend for marketing.

Amazon (affiliate links)

When you click an Amazon affiliate link on this site, Amazon.com, Inc. may set cookies on your device and collect information about your visit in order to attribute a qualifying purchase to our account. This is governed by Amazon's own privacy notice: Amazon Privacy Notice.

Social sharing platforms

When you use the share button on a product, your browser opens a page on the platform you choose — Twitter/X, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, or WhatsApp. Those platforms may set their own cookies and collect information about your visit under their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not receive any personal information from those platforms.

Image hosts

Product images are served from third-party image hosting services (such as ImgBB). Those providers may log the request when your browser loads an image.

Hosting provider

Our website is hosted on a third-party platform (GitHub Pages) that keeps standard access logs for security and operational purposes.

5Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to create your account, authenticate you, and provide the community features you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — for server logs, security, spam prevention, moderation, and understanding aggregate traffic.
  • Consent — where required, for any non-essential cookies set by third parties. We do not currently set any non-essential cookies ourselves.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose information by law, including responding to valid legal requests.

6Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — delete your account from your settings page, or ask us to delete specific data.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email the.whacky.rack@limbomail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days).

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from your settings page. When you do:

  • Your profile is soft-deleted: your username becomes [deleted]_xxxxxxxx and your bio, avatar, country, gender, and age are erased.
  • Your profile page stops being publicly viewable.
  • Your Yaps, Tapes, and comments remain visible (so discussions don't break) but are attributed to [deleted]_xxxxxxxx.
  • Your session is cleared and you cannot sign back in.

If you want your content removed too, email us after deleting your account and we will process it on request.

Session tokens and anonymous IDs

Your session token and the anonymous like identifier are stored in your browser's localStorage. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings, which will sign you out and reset your anonymous likes on this device. This is a browser-level operation and does not delete your account.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do neither), and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

7Children's privacy

The Whacky Rack is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8International data transfers

Our service providers (hosting, Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, Amazon, image hosts, and any social platform you choose to share to) may store or process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, these providers rely on approved transfer mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9Data retention

  • Account data (email, hashed password, username, rank, join date) is retained while your account is active.
  • Profile data (bio, country, gender, age, avatar, timezone) is retained while your account is active and is erased when you delete your account.
  • User-generated content (Yaps, Tapes, comments, votes, reactions, bookmarks, follows) is retained while your account is active. After you delete your account, content is kept but attributed to [deleted]_xxxxxxxx, unless you separately request its removal.
  • Soft-deleted content (content removed by its author or by a moderator that has replies underneath it) is displayed as [deleted] but kept in the database to preserve thread structure.
  • Reports and moderation logs are retained so we can review abuse patterns and respond to legal requests.
  • Server logs are retained by our hosting and backend providers according to their standard retention schedules.
  • Transactional email logs (Resend) are retained only as long as needed to confirm delivery.
  • Anonymous like records are retained indefinitely so product like counts remain accurate. The identifier associated with a like is anonymous and cannot be linked to an individual.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect any information we handle. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.
