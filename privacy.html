Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information The Whacky Rack collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't sell your data, and we don't ask you to create an account.

Last updated: 2026

Plain-English summary

The Whacky Rack does not have user accounts, logins, comment sections, or a newsletter. We don't collect names, emails, or payment details. If you use the like button, we store an anonymous identifier and your likes in your browser's localStorage and in our database — no personal information is involved. The only third parties that may set cookies on your device are Amazon (when you click an affiliate link) and a social platform (when you click a share button). That's it.

1Who we are

The Whacky Rack ("we", "us", "our") is an independently operated editorial website that publishes curated product recommendations and factual stories from around the world. We are the data controller for any personal information collected through this site.

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the.whacky.rack@limbomail.com.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

We only receive personal information if you voluntarily send it to us — typically by emailing our contact address. In that case we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it solely to reply to you.

Information collected automatically

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit — IP address, browser type, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. This is standard server-log data used for security, debugging, and understanding aggregate traffic patterns. It is not linked to any identifiable individual by us.

Information stored when you like a product

When you use the like button on a product, two pieces of information are stored in your browser's localStorage:

  • A randomly generated identifier (a UUID) that has no connection to your name, email, or any other personal information.
  • A list of the product IDs you have liked, so the heart icon stays filled when you return to the site.

When you like a product, we also send that random identifier and the product ID to our database (hosted on Supabase) so that the like can be counted and displayed. No personal information is transmitted — only the anonymous identifier and the product ID.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or offer account registration.
  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, or postal addresses.
  • We do not collect payment information — we don't sell anything directly.
  • We do not operate a newsletter or mailing list at this time.
  • We do not use fingerprinting, session recording, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • We do not collect your identity through the like feature — the identifier we use is random and cannot be traced back to you.

3How we use information

The limited information we receive is used only to:

  • Respond to messages you send us.
  • Keep the website secure and functioning (server logs).
  • Understand aggregate, anonymised traffic patterns.
  • Display like counts on product cards.
  • Comply with legal obligations if required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

4Third parties who may receive data

Amazon (affiliate links)

When you click an Amazon affiliate link on this site, Amazon.com, Inc. may set cookies on your device and collect information about your visit in order to attribute a qualifying purchase to our account. This is governed by Amazon's own privacy notice: Amazon Privacy Notice.

Supabase (content delivery and likes)

Our product and story data is hosted on Supabase. When your browser loads content from Supabase, Supabase may log the request as part of normal infrastructure operation. When you like a product, the anonymous identifier described in section 2 and the product ID are stored in a Supabase-hosted database. We do not use Supabase authentication, so no session or account cookies are set on your device by our use of it.

Social sharing platforms

When you use the share button on a product, your browser opens a page on the platform you choose — Twitter/X, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, or WhatsApp. Those platforms may set their own cookies and collect information about your visit under their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not receive any personal information from those platforms, and we do not know which products you share or with whom.

Image hosts

Product images are served from third-party image hosting services (such as ImgBB). Those providers may log the request when your browser loads an image.

Hosting provider

Our website is hosted on a third-party platform that keeps standard access logs for security and operational purposes.

5Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — for server logs, security, and preventing abuse.
  • Consent — where required, for any non-essential cookies set by third parties.
  • Contract — where you have contacted us and we need to respond.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

Because the like feature stores only an anonymous identifier and a list of product IDs, and does not track you across other sites, we treat it as a functional, user-initiated feature rather than tracking.

6Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email the.whacky.rack@limbomail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days).

Because the like identifier is stored only in your browser and is not linked to your identity, you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data for this site. This will also clear any likes you have registered on this device.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do neither), and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

7Children's privacy

The Whacky Rack is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8International data transfers

Our service providers (hosting, Supabase, Amazon, image hosts, and any social platform you choose to share to) may store or process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, these providers rely on approved transfer mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9Data retention

We retain email correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the matter at hand, after which it is deleted. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider according to their standard retention schedule. Like records in our database are retained indefinitely so that like counts remain accurate over time; the identifier associated with a like is anonymous and cannot be linked to an individual.

10Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect any information we handle. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11External links

Our site contains links to external websites, including Amazon, reference sources for our editorial content, and the social platforms you may share to. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
