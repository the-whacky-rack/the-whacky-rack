Vendor Terms

These terms apply to every independent vendor who lists products on The Side Rack, The Whacky Rack's marketplace for approved makers. Read them carefully — by submitting an application, you agree to them.

Last updated: 2026

Plain-English summary

Listing on The Side Rack is free. We don't take commission. You keep 100% of every sale. Your store stays your store — we simply link to it. In return, we ask you to list honestly, describe products accurately, fulfil orders promptly, handle refunds and complaints fairly, respect your customers' data, and follow the laws of your country and the countries you sell to. We review every product before it goes live. We can remove listings or suspend vendors who break these rules. You can withdraw at any time.

1Acceptance of these terms

These Vendor Terms ("Vendor Terms") govern your listing and sale of products through The Side Rack (the "Platform"), operated by The Whacky Rack ("we", "us", "our"). They are incorporated into and form part of our Terms of Service. By submitting a vendor application or listing a product, you agree to be bound by these Vendor Terms.

If you do not agree with any part of these Vendor Terms, do not apply to become a vendor, and do not list any products.

2Eligibility

To be eligible to become a Side Rack vendor, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into contracts in your country.
  • Be a small business, an independent maker, or a sole trader — not a large corporation.
  • Be located in one of the countries currently supported by The Side Rack (see our Privacy Policy or ask us if you're unsure).
  • Have your own website or online store where customers can purchase your products. We do not host checkout, we link out to you.
  • Have a valid contact email address that you monitor regularly.
  • Comply with all laws applicable to your business, including consumer protection, tax, and product safety laws.

3Application and approval

How to apply

You apply through our vendor application form. You must provide accurate information about your business, including a business name, a working website URL, a contact email, your country, a short bio, whether you are a small business, and a statement of why you wish to be listed.

Admin review

Every application is reviewed by a human. We may approve, reject, or ask for more information. We do not guarantee approval.

Re-application

If your application is rejected, you may re-apply a maximum of 5 times, with a minimum gap of 7 days between each attempt. After 5 rejections, we reserve the right to permanently block your application.

4What you may list

You may list products that:

  • You sell on your own website (the link must go to your own store).
  • Are accurately described, correctly categorised, and pictured as they will be delivered.
  • Fit the vibe of The Whacky Rack — unusual, handmade, quirky, or otherwise interesting.
  • Fall into one of the categories listed on The Side Rack.

5What you may not list

Breaking these rules will result in removal of the listing, suspension of your vendor account, and possible permanent ban.

You may not list or link to any product that:

  • Is illegal in your country, the countries you ship to, or the countries where the product is viewed.
  • Is counterfeit, replica, or in any way infringes another party's intellectual property.
  • Contains hate speech, discrimination, harassment, or threats.
  • Is sexually explicit, obscene, or pornographic.
  • Depicts or promotes violence, self-harm, or terrorism.
  • Is a weapon, ammunition, explosive, or controlled substance.
  • Is a pharmaceutical, medical device, or supplement making unsubstantiated health claims.
  • Is a tobacco, alcohol, vaping, or cannabis product where prohibited.
  • Is a lottery, gambling service, or financial product.
  • Is a live animal or a product made from an endangered species.
  • Infringes privacy rights — for example, personal data without consent.
  • Uses a URL shortener or Amazon affiliate link (we do not allow either).
  • Is part of a drop-shipping operation with no identifiable maker or brand.

This list is not exhaustive. We may remove any listing that we reasonably believe harms other users, our Platform, or violates applicable law, even if it is not explicitly on this list.

6Your commitments as a vendor

By listing on The Side Rack, you commit to the following:

Accuracy

  • Every product listing will accurately describe the item, its materials, its condition, and its price.
  • You will not make false claims about origin, quality, safety, or efficacy.
  • You will update or remove listings when products become unavailable.
  • Your bio and business information will be truthful and kept up to date.

Fulfilment

  • You will dispatch orders within the timeframe you state on your own store.
  • You will ship to the countries you claim to ship to.
  • You will provide order confirmation, tracking where applicable, and delivery updates.
  • You will resolve delivery failures promptly — either by reshipping or refunding.

Refunds, returns, and complaints

  • You will honour your own published return and refund policy, and any statutory rights your customers have.
  • You will respond to customer enquiries within a reasonable time — we suggest 2 business days.
  • You will resolve complaints fairly. If you cannot, we may intervene.

Customer service

  • You will not harass, threaten, or abuse any customer, whether they contact you through The Side Rack, your store, or elsewhere.
  • You will not use information you learn about a customer to send them unsolicited marketing.

Data protection

  • You will comply with all data protection laws applicable to you when you handle customer data (GDPR, UK GDPR, DPDP, CCPA/CPRA, Australian Privacy Act, or others).
  • You will maintain your own privacy policy and honour the rights it grants.
  • You will not ask us for customer data — we do not share any with you.
  • You will not use the Platform to scrape or harvest data about visitors, users, or other vendors.

Platform integrity

  • You will not manipulate reviews, likes, clicks, or analytics on your own or anyone else's listings.
  • You will not create multiple vendor accounts to evade limits or bans.
  • You will not use bots or automated tools to interact with the Platform.
  • You will not attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Platform.

7Our commitments

In return, we commit to:

  • Review every application and every new product listing by hand.
  • Communicate decisions via the notification system in your vendor portal.
  • Give a clear reason if we reject an application or a product.
  • Not charge you any fee for listing or selling through The Side Rack.
  • Not take any commission on your sales.
  • Not alter your listing content without your knowledge, other than cosmetic formatting consistent with Platform layout.
  • Give you reasonable notice before removing a listing, except where immediate removal is required by law or to protect other users.
  • Preserve the confidentiality of your contact email and any information you did not intend to make public.

8Product limit and edits

Default limit

Every vendor is limited to 10 active listings by default. This limit exists to keep the Platform curated and to prevent abuse. If you have a legitimate reason to list more, contact us — we may increase your limit on a case-by-case basis.

Editing live listings

You may edit any of your live listings at any time. However, to protect customers and the Platform:

  • Cosmetic changes (image, price note, shipping note) go live immediately.
  • Substantive changes (product name, link URL, category) trigger re-review by an admin. The listing stays live while under review.
  • If the re-reviewed listing is rejected, it will be removed and you will be notified with a reason.

9Suspension and termination

When we may suspend or terminate you

We may suspend or terminate your vendor account at any time, with or without notice, for reasons including:

  • Violation of these Vendor Terms or our Terms of Service.
  • Repeated complaints from customers that we cannot resolve with you.
  • Fraudulent, misleading, or illegal activity.
  • Failure to respond to Platform communications for an extended period.
  • Request by a law enforcement or regulatory authority.
  • Technical or security reasons.

What happens when you are suspended

  • Your listings are hidden from The Side Rack immediately.
  • Existing listings remain in our database for audit purposes and may be reinstated if you are restored.
  • You may appeal by contacting our Grievance Officer.

Withdrawing voluntarily

You may stop being a vendor at any time by contacting us. We will hide your listings and close your vendor account. Your products will not appear on The Side Rack after withdrawal.
